# 💫 About Me:

I have hands-on experience building scalable and user-centric solutions 🚀, with a focus on **RESTful APIs** 🔗, **database integration** 🗄️, and clean, maintainable code.

My academic and project work also covers **Software Project Management and Quality Engineering** 📋, where I have applied **agile methodologies** 🔁, quality assurance practices ✔️, and effective **version control using Git/GitHub** 🧩.

Furthermore, I possess knowledge of **Business Process Engineering** 🏗️, enabling me to analyze, optimize, and align technical solutions with business objectives 📈. I am a proactive **team player** 🤝 with strong **problem-solving skills** 🧠 and a passion for **collaborative, agile-driven development** ⚡.
